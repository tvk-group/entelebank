# EnteleBANK Regulatory Business Plan v1.0

**Status:** Working draft — internal regulatory preparation  
**Entity:** Proposed ENTELEBANK LTD  
**Brand:** EnteleBANK — A TVK Group Company  
**Regulatory status:** Not authorised by the PRA or FCA to accept deposits or provide regulated banking services.

> This document is a development artefact for a prospective UK bank. It does not evidence PRA/FCA approval, authorisation, permission, endorsement or entry into pre-application engagement.

## 1. Executive proposition
EnteleBANK is being developed as a digital-first UK banking institution intended initially to serve UK retail consumers, entrepreneurs, professionals, SMEs and corporate customers. The target proposition is secure, understandable personal and business banking supported by resilient payments and modern financial infrastructure.

The institution will not commence deposit-taking or another regulated banking activity requiring authorisation unless and until the relevant permissions are obtained.

## 2. Why a bank
The intended long-term model includes accepting deposits and providing banking products where prudential safety, customer protection, governance and balance-sheet responsibility are core to the proposition. The project must demonstrate why a bank model is appropriate rather than relying on an EMI, payment institution or unregulated technology-company perimeter.

**Evidence required:** documented perimeter analysis comparing bank / EMI / PI alternatives and explaining the need for deposit-taking permission.

## 3. Target customers and markets
Initial target market: United Kingdom.

Prospective customer segments:
- retail consumers;
- professionals and entrepreneurs;
- SMEs;
- selected corporate customers.

Expansion outside the UK is not assumed in the base case and would require separate legal, regulatory, operational and financial analysis.

## 4. Proposed products
Subject to authorisation, permissions and product governance:
- personal current accounts;
- savings accounts;
- business current accounts;
- domestic payments and transfers;
- debit cards;
- Direct Debits and Faster Payments connectivity/participation strategy;
- international payments;
- foreign exchange and multi-currency capabilities;
- selected business treasury capabilities.

No product described here is currently offered by EnteleBANK.

## 5. Customer journey and Consumer Duty
The target journey must cover acquisition, eligibility, onboarding, identity verification, account opening, authentication, servicing, payments, fraud events, complaints, vulnerability/support needs, account restriction/closure and offboarding.

Design principles: fair value, understandable communications, appropriate support, avoidance of foreseeable harm, measurable customer outcomes and accessible servicing.

## 6. Ownership, controllers and group structure
Target high-level structure: TVK Group / controlling shareholder(s) → ENTELEBANK LTD.

Before regulatory engagement the project must establish and evidence:
- exact legal ownership and percentages;
- ultimate beneficial owners and controllers;
- source of wealth and source of funds;
- group entities and intra-group dependencies;
- related-party arrangements;
- conflicts of interest;
- consolidated-supervision implications;
- a simple capital/share structure appropriate to prudential assessment.

## 7. Governance and senior management
Target governance must provide effective Board oversight, independent challenge and clear executive accountability.

Required workstreams:
- proposed Board composition and skills matrix;
- Chair and independent non-executive requirements;
- CEO/CFO/CRO/Compliance/MLRO and other relevant senior responsibilities;
- committee architecture;
- delegated authorities;
- conflicts framework;
- succession and key-person risk;
- SMCR mapping and fitness/propriety evidence.

## 8. Risk management framework
Develop Board-approved risk appetite and taxonomy covering at minimum:
- capital;
- liquidity and funding;
- credit;
- market/FX;
- operational resilience;
- technology and cyber;
- model/AI;
- third-party/outsourcing;
- financial crime;
- conduct and Consumer Duty;
- legal/regulatory;
- strategic/business model;
- reputational risk.

Adopt proportionate three-lines responsibilities with documented ownership, monitoring, escalation and independent assurance.

## 9. Financial crime framework
Design an end-to-end framework covering customer risk assessment, CDD/EDD, beneficial ownership, PEPs, sanctions, transaction monitoring, fraud, suspicious activity escalation/reporting, correspondent/payment risks, proliferation-financing considerations, record keeping, training, quality assurance and independent testing.

No customer onboarding should occur until the applicable control environment is authorised, operational and tested.

## 10. Capital, liquidity and funding
Build a five-year integrated financial model with monthly granularity for the early period and explicit assumptions for customers, balances, deposits, revenues, costs, losses and growth.

Required prudential work includes capital planning, liquidity/funding strategy, stress scenarios, ICAAP/ILAAP development, capital instruments assessment, recovery indicators and funding evidence.

The project must not represent an assumed regulatory capital requirement as an approved PRA requirement.

## 11. Technology and security
Target principles:
- zero/least privilege;
- strong identity and privileged-access management;
- encryption and managed keys;
- separation of duties;
- secure SDLC and reproducible builds;
- software supply-chain controls;
- vulnerability management;
- immutable/auditable security evidence;
- logging, monitoring and detection;
- backup/recovery testing;
- data classification and retention;
- incident response;
- resilience-by-design.

The public website is not a banking system and must remain segregated from future customer/core-banking environments.

## 12. Operational resilience
Identify important business services, impact tolerances, people/process/technology/facility/third-party dependencies, scenario testing, business continuity, disaster recovery, communications and lessons-learned governance.

## 13. Outsourcing and third parties
Create an inventory and materiality assessment for proposed core banking, cloud, identity, payment, card, communications, security and data providers. Contracts must support security, audit/access, data location, resilience, incident notification, subcontracting control and exit/portability requirements appropriate to the service.

## 14. Data protection and records
Develop UK GDPR/data-protection governance, records of processing, lawful bases, minimisation, retention, data-subject rights, DPIAs, international transfer controls and regulatory record retention.

## 15. Recovery and solvent exit
Develop recovery planning, recovery options, indicators, governance and communications. Build solvent-exit analysis and execution planning appropriate to the prospective bank's business model and dependencies.

## 16. Mobilisation strategy
Mobilisation/Authorisation with Restrictions is an option to be evaluated, not an assumed entitlement. The project must decide whether it can be fully operational at authorisation or whether a credible mobilisation route is preferable.

Any mobilisation plan must be substantially developed before authorisation and should identify remaining actions, owners, funding, dependencies, evidence and exit criteria.

## 17. Regulatory engagement plan
Preparation sequence:
1. finalise proposition and why-bank analysis;
2. resolve ownership/controllers and funding strategy;
3. build credible RBP and financial model;
4. establish governance and risk architecture;
5. prepare pre-application materials;
6. seek early engagement through the PRA/FCA New Bank Start-Up Unit route when sufficiently ready;
7. incorporate challenge and feedback before deciding when to submit a formal application.

## 18. Consistency and change control
The RBP, financial model, website, application forms, policies, ICAAP/ILAAP and regulatory correspondence must tell the same factual story. Material changes must be documented, challenged and propagated across affected artefacts.

While the FCA Sensitive Business Name request for ENTELEBANK LTD remains pending, public communications must comply with repository Issue #9 and must not create an impression that EnteleBANK is already an authorised bank.
