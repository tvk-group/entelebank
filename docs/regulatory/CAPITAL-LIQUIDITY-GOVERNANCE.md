# EnteleBANK Capital & Liquidity Governance Framework v0.1

**Status:** Internal working framework — pre-authorisation

## Purpose
Define how capital, liquidity and funding adequacy will be governed, modelled, challenged and evidenced as EnteleBANK progresses through pre-application and any future authorisation process.

## Principles
- Maintain resources appropriate to the business model, risks and stage of development.
- Separate regulatory minimums from management buffers and contingency headroom.
- Integrate capital/liquidity forecasts with the five-year business model and stress scenarios.
- Do not treat uncommitted group or investor support as available funding.
- Escalate emerging shortfalls early and preserve credible management actions.

## Capital governance
The future framework must map applicable prudential requirements, own-funds composition/quality, forecast risk-weighted exposures, operating-loss runway, management buffers, stress impacts and capital actions. Numerical requirements remain pending regulatory perimeter, model and evidence.

## Liquidity and funding
The framework must identify funding sources, concentration, maturity, currency, behavioural assumptions, liquid-asset strategy, cash-flow needs, intraday/payment liquidity where applicable and contingency funding options. Assumptions must be reconciled to the RBP and financial model.

## Stress testing
Base, Downside, Severe-but-Plausible and Reverse-Stress scenarios must quantify impacts on capital, liquidity, profitability/runway and customer/service outcomes. Management actions require feasibility, timing and dependency evidence.

## Governance and MI
Board/executive oversight, limits, early-warning indicators, escalation, Treasury/Finance responsibilities, independent Risk challenge and assurance must be defined before application readiness. Named roles remain evidence-gated.

## Contingency funding
A future contingency funding plan must define triggers, governance, liquidity actions, communication and operational execution. No facility or group support is assumed unless legally documented and credible.

## Model controls
All prudential model inputs require source, owner, date, approval status and sensitivity. Changes must be version-controlled and reconciled across RBP, proposition deck, recovery/exit and regulatory submissions.

## Sensitive-name boundary
This internal framework does not imply that EnteleBANK is authorised, capitalised to a regulatory requirement, accepting deposits or providing regulated banking services. Issue #9 remains release-blocking.
