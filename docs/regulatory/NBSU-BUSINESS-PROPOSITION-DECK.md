# EnteleBANK — NBSU Business Proposition Deck

Status: Internal working deck structure. Not evidence of PRA/FCA engagement or approval.

## Slide 1 — EnteleBANK
A TVK Group Company
Proposed UK digital banking institution — pre-authorisation.

## Slide 2 — Why become a bank?
Explain why the intended long-term proposition requires a deposit-taking bank rather than merely a software, payment-institution or e-money model. This must be tied to actual intended regulated activities and customer value, not brand prestige.

## Slide 3 — Problem and target customers
Initial UK focus: retail consumers, entrepreneurs, professionals, SMEs and selected corporate customers, subject to refinement from targeted market research. Define specific underserved needs with evidence.

## Slide 4 — Proposed initial proposition
Prospective current/savings accounts, domestic payments/transfers, debit-card capability, business banking and other services only where within approved scope. Clearly separate Day-1 scope from later phases.

## Slide 5 — Customer journey and Consumer Duty
Discovery → eligibility → onboarding/KYC → account servicing → payments → support → complaints/vulnerability support → exit. Map foreseeable harm and controls at each stage.

## Slide 6 — Business model and route to sustainability
Revenue drivers, cost base, acquisition strategy, balance-sheet strategy, funding dependency and prudent route to profitability. Insert only model-governed numbers.

## Slide 7 — Five-year financial outlook
High-level balance sheet, income statement, capital/liquidity metrics and runway for Years 1–5. Present Base plus downside sensitivity. Numbers remain pending until model evidence gates close.

## Slide 8 — Ownership, controllers and funding
Show legal ownership chain, proposed controllers, source of wealth/funds, committed/conditional funding and future capital plan. No percentage or funding claim without evidence.

## Slide 9 — Governance and senior management
Proposed Board/committee architecture, SMF coverage, three lines, independent challenge and recruitment gaps. Do not present unappointed people as office holders.

## Slide 10 — Risk profile
Top inherent risks: strategic/business model, capital, liquidity, operational resilience, cyber, financial crime, conduct, third party, data/privacy, fraud and any credit/market risk created by final products.

## Slide 11 — Financial crime framework
Enterprise-wide risk assessment, KYC/KYB, beneficial ownership, sanctions/PEP, transaction monitoring, fraud controls, investigations, SAR governance, assurance and MI.

## Slide 12 — Technology and operational resilience
Core banking architecture, IAM, encryption, secure SDLC, observability, dependency management, cloud/vendor controls, important business services, impact tolerances, BCP/DR and testing.

## Slide 13 — Outsourcing and third parties
Material providers, substitutability, concentration, data locations, exit plans, contractual/audit rights and operational-resilience dependencies. Vendor names only after selection/diligence.

## Slide 14 — Regulatory programme
Sensitive-name request is separate from bank authorisation. Show internal preparation → optional NBSU pre-application → detailed RBP/technical sessions → complete application → assessment → possible mobilisation or unrestricted authorisation. Do not imply current progression beyond factual evidence.

## Slide 15 — Programme plan and key dependencies
Entity/name, controllers/funding, Board/SMFs, market research, model, policies, vendors, build/testing, ICAAP/ILAAP, recovery/solvent exit, application evidence and independent review.

## Slide 16 — Decisions/questions for NBSU
Use the meeting to test regulatory perimeter, proposed scope, mobilisation strategy, ownership/group structure, capital/liquidity approach, governance plan, technology/outsourcing approach and evidence expectations.

## Presentation controls
- Every slide carries “Proposed / pre-authorisation” where status could otherwise be misunderstood.
- One RBP source of truth for regulators, prospective investors and internal governance.
- Deck numbers reconcile to the controlled five-year model.
- Public website claims are not copied into the deck unless verified; deck claims are not copied to the public website without Issue #9 review.
