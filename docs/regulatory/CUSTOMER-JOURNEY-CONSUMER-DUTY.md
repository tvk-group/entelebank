# EnteleBANK — Customer Journey & Consumer Duty Workstream

**Status:** Working regulatory design — not a live customer process  
**Public availability:** None  
**Sensitive-name guardrail:** Issue #9 applies.

## Purpose
Design the prospective retail customer journey so conduct risk, financial crime controls, accessibility, support and Consumer Duty outcomes are considered from inception rather than added after product build.

## Target journey
1. **Discovery** — clear audience, eligibility and prospective-product communications; no misleading authorisation/FSCS claims.
2. **Pre-contract information** — understandable features, limitations, pricing and key risks before commitment.
3. **Application** — accessible digital journey with alternative support paths.
4. **Identity/KYC** — proportionate identity, sanctions, PEP and financial-crime controls with escalation and false-positive handling.
5. **Decision** — explainable outcomes, appropriate human review and fair treatment where automation supports decisions.
6. **Account activation** — only after applicable authorisation and operational readiness; security setup and fraud education.
7. **Everyday use** — secure payments, alerts, transaction transparency, service availability and support.
8. **Vulnerability/support** — identification of support needs, accessible channels and appropriate adjustments.
9. **Complaints/remediation** — accessible complaint intake, root-cause analysis, redress governance and management information.
10. **Exit/closure** — fair notice, safe funds transfer and clear records, subject to legal/financial-crime constraints.

## Consumer Duty outcome design
### Products & services
- defined target market and negative target market;
- product approval and periodic review;
- foreseeable-harm assessment;
- distribution-channel controls.

### Price & value
- evidence-based fair-value assessment before launch;
- fees, interest and FX economics assessed against customer benefits and costs;
- monitoring for cohorts receiving poor value.

### Consumer understanding
- layered, plain-language communications;
- comprehension testing for material disclosures;
- no dark patterns or urgency manipulation;
- translated content controlled against authoritative English.

### Consumer support
- support standards appropriate to channel and customer need;
- vulnerability and accessibility pathways;
- complaint and fraud escalation;
- monitoring abandonment, wait times, repeat contacts and unresolved cases.

## Conduct risk evidence required before application
- target-market analysis;
- end-to-end journey maps;
- conduct risk assessment;
- Consumer Duty implementation plan;
- product governance policy;
- vulnerable-customer policy;
- complaints policy and MI design;
- fair-value methodology;
- customer communications inventory;
- outcome-testing plan.

## Release boundary
This document does not mean EnteleBANK currently accepts applications, opens accounts or provides regulated banking services.
