# EnteleBANK — Payment-System Access Decision Framework

**Status:** Working design; no provider selected or contracted.

## Regulatory planning principle
A prospective UK bank needs a credible path to payment-system access. EnteleBANK will assess access early enough for the chosen route to be reflected consistently in the RBP, technology architecture, outsourcing register, operational resilience mapping, financial model and mobilisation plan.

## Routes to assess
### Full direct access
Direct technical and settlement relationship with the relevant payment-system operator. Assess settlement-account requirements, certification, scheme obligations, liquidity, operations, resilience, security and cost.

### Direct technical / sponsored settlement
Direct technical connection with settlement sponsorship. Assess split responsibilities, sponsor dependency, settlement/liquidity model, incident ownership and exit arrangements.

### Indirect access
An indirect access provider sponsors technical and settlement access. Assess concentration risk, service levels, safeguarding of operational dependencies, pricing, data flows, financial crime responsibilities, resilience, substitution and exit.

## Systems in scope for strategy
- Faster Payments / future UK retail payments infrastructure;
- Bacs where relevant to payroll/direct-credit/direct-debit proposition;
- CHAPS for high-value sterling needs if justified by target market;
- card scheme/issuer-processing architecture as a separate workstream;
- cross-border correspondent/payment-provider model where justified.

Listing a system here is not a decision to join it.

## Decision scorecard
Each option must be scored for: customer proposition fit; regulatory/control clarity; time to readiness; implementation complexity; capital/liquidity implications; operational resilience; cyber/security; financial crime; vendor/concentration risk; unit economics; scalability; exit/substitutability.

## Evidence gate
No provider, sponsor, scheme membership, launch date or access route may be represented as selected until due diligence, commercial evidence, technical feasibility and governance approval exist.
