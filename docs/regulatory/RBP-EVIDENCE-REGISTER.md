# EnteleBANK — RBP Evidence & Assumption Register

This register prevents unsupported statements from entering the Regulatory Business Plan, financial model, NBSU proposition or public website.

| Area | Evidence required | Status | Owner | Public? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sensitive name | FCA non-objection | Pending | Regulatory | No approval claim |
| Legal entity | Companies House incorporation evidence after non-objection/registration | Pending | Corporate | Only once factual |
| Ownership | Full legal ownership chart and percentages | Pending | Corporate | No |
| Controllers | Controller analysis, identity, fitness/propriety, source of wealth/funds | Pending | Regulatory | No |
| Initial funding | Bank statements/commitment/subscription evidence and source of funds | Pending | Finance | No |
| Market need | Targeted UK market research and competitor analysis | Pending | Strategy | Summaries only after review |
| Product scope | Day-1 and phased product/perimeter decision | Working | Product/Regulatory | Proposed only |
| Pricing | Approved pricing and fair-value assessment | Pending | Product/Finance | No |
| Customer forecasts | Model assumptions supported by research | Pending | Finance/Strategy | No |
| Financial forecasts | Controlled five-year model + challenge | Pending | Finance | No |
| Capital/liquidity | Applicable regulatory methodology/requirements | Pending | Finance/Risk | No |
| Board/SMFs | Appointment/recruitment evidence | Pending | Governance | Only factual appointments |
| Vendors | Selection, diligence, contracts, resilience/exit assessment | Pending | Technology/Ops | No unsupported names |
| Security | Architecture, testing, risk treatment and assurance evidence | Working | Security | High-level only |
| Financial crime | EWRA/framework/policies/MI design | Working | Compliance | High-level only |
| Operational resilience | IBS, impact tolerances, mapping/testing | Pending | Ops/Risk | High-level only |
| Translation | Complete catalogue + linguistic/regulatory review | Working | Content/Regulatory | Only approved locales |

## Rule
“Pending” is not a weakness to conceal. It is a control state. Any material assertion lacking evidence stays explicitly pending rather than being estimated, inferred or invented.
