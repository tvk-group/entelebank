# EnteleBANK — Ownership, Funding & Five-Year Model Framework

Status: Working regulatory document — evidence gated  
Public use: No  
Sensitive-name guardrail: Issue #9 applies

## 1. Purpose

This document turns the Regulatory Business Plan into an evidence workstream for ownership, controllers, funding and the five-year financial model. No percentage, capital amount, valuation, regulatory requirement or profitability date may be inserted without documentary support and internal challenge.

## 2. Ownership and controller evidence pack

Before pre-application engagement, maintain a verified record of:

- proposed legal shareholder(s) and complete ownership chain;
- ultimate beneficial owners and persons exercising significant influence/control;
- proposed voting, dividend and other economic rights;
- source of wealth and source of funds evidence for relevant controllers/investors;
- group entities, intra-group dependencies and related-party arrangements;
- conflicts of interest and mitigation;
- controller financial capacity to support the institution through application, mobilisation (if used) and early operation;
- any shareholder agreement, side agreement, option, convertible, preference right or other instrument capable of changing control/economics.

Design principle: keep the proposed bank share structure as simple and transparent as practicable. Any complexity requires a documented regulatory and commercial rationale.

## 3. Group perimeter

The proposed bank entity must have a clearly defined perimeter. Wider TVK Group / EnteleKRON ecosystem activities do not automatically become activities of the prospective bank.

For each group relationship record:

| Relationship | Evidence required | Key risk question |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership/control | Corporate records and cap table | Who ultimately controls the bank? |
| Funding | Agreements and source-of-funds evidence | Is funding credible, clean and available when required? |
| Shared technology | Architecture, contracts, SLAs | Can the bank remain operational if a group service fails? |
| Shared people | Roles, time allocation, conflicts | Are bank responsibilities sufficiently independent? |
| Brand/IP | Licence/ownership terms | Can the bank continue using critical IP under stress? |
| Data | Data-flow maps and agreements | Are customer/regulatory data boundaries controlled? |
| Digital assets | Separate perimeter assessment | Could group crypto activity create prudential/conduct/reputational contagion? |

## 4. Funding stages

Model funding separately for:

1. pre-application development;
2. application and regulatory assessment;
3. mobilisation, if that route is selected;
4. unrestricted launch readiness;
5. years 1–5 after authorisation.

For every funding source record amount, currency, timing, legal form, conditions, committed/uncommitted status, source of funds, dilution/control impact and downside availability.

## 5. Five-year model architecture

The financial model will contain linked monthly assumptions for at least the build/mobilisation period and early operating period, with annual summaries for Years 1–5.

### Customer and volume drivers
- active personal customers;
- active business customers;
- account openings/closures;
- average deposits by segment;
- payment volumes;
- card volumes if in scope;
- FX/multi-currency volumes if in scope;
- customer acquisition cost and retention assumptions.

### Income
- net interest income;
- payment/card income where applicable;
- FX income where applicable;
- account/service income where applicable;
- other income only where legally and operationally credible.

### Costs
- people and governance;
- core banking/cloud/security;
- payments/card schemes and processors;
- KYC/AML/sanctions/fraud tooling;
- professional/regulatory/audit costs;
- customer operations;
- insurance;
- premises/administration;
- outsourcing and critical third parties;
- contingency and remediation.

### Balance sheet / prudential drivers
- customer deposits;
- cash and liquid assets;
- lending only if/when approved in scope;
- fixed/intangible assets;
- capital instruments;
- regulatory capital and liquidity requirements once determined through the appropriate process.

## 6. Scenario set

At minimum maintain:

- Base case — evidence-backed management case;
- Downside — slower acquisition, lower deposits/revenue, higher operating cost;
- Severe-but-plausible — material operational/revenue/funding stress;
- Reverse stress — conditions that make the model non-viable and management actions required before that point.

## 7. Model governance

Every material assumption requires: owner, source/evidence, date, rationale, sensitivity, approval status and version history. The RBP narrative and financial model must never contain conflicting assumptions.

## 8. Current unresolved decisions

Do not fill by assumption:

- final shareholder/controller percentages;
- initial paid-in capital and total committed funding;
- mobilisation versus direct authorisation strategy;
- lending scope/timing;
- pricing and interest rates;
- customer acquisition forecasts;
- profitability/break-even date;
- capital/liquidity requirements.

These are decision/evidence gates, not blanks to be cosmetically completed.
