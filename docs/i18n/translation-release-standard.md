# EnteleBANK — Translation Release Standard

Applies to all 25 configured website languages.

## Principle

English is the authoritative regulatory source during the pre-authorisation period. A locale must not be presented as complete merely because a route exists.

## Release gates for each language

A locale may be activated as complete only when all public content in scope has:

1. complete human-quality translation;
2. terminology consistency for PRA, FCA, authorisation, deposits, regulated services, FSCS and prospective/proposed status;
3. preservation of the exact legal meaning of pre-authorisation disclaimers;
4. no accidental translation of EnteleBANK, TVK Group, PRA, FCA or other protected/proper names;
5. navigation, metadata, accessibility labels, footer, legal pages and error states translated;
6. RTL review for Arabic, Persian and Hebrew;
7. link/route validation;
8. second-person review of regulatory/legal text;
9. version/date recorded against the English source revision;
10. approval in the translation completeness register.

## Mandatory translated disclaimer concept

Every active locale must clearly communicate that EnteleBANK is a proposed UK banking institution under development, is not currently authorised by the PRA/FCA to accept deposits or provide regulated banking services, and will not commence regulated activity requiring authorisation unless/until the required authorisations and permissions are obtained.

## Authoritative-language notice

Translated regulatory/legal pages must state that the English version is authoritative if a translation conflicts with it during the pre-authorisation period.

## No runtime machine translation

Do not use browser/runtime automated translation APIs to generate regulatory or legal website copy. Translation catalogues are version-controlled content.
