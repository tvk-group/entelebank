# EnteleBANK

**A TVK Group Company**

Official pre-authorisation website for the proposed UK banking institution EnteleBANK.

## Regulatory status

EnteleBANK is under development and is not currently authorised by the PRA or FCA to accept deposits or provide regulated banking services. No deposit-taking or regulated banking activity requiring authorisation will commence unless and until the required regulatory authorisations and permissions have been obtained.
